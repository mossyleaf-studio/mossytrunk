# Product report (Bilan produits)

`/reports/products` (Bureau › « Bilan produits ») tells how the catalogue sold over a period, without touching the product pages.

| # | Rule | Where | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| PR1 | The period is « 12 derniers mois » (from the first day of the month eleven months ago to today, the default), a calendar year, or « Depuis le début » (from the first sale); days in Europe/Paris. Refunded orders are left out | `ReportPeriod` | `ProductReportsTest` |
| PR2 | **Palmarès**: every article sold in the period with its units, its sales at full price, its revenue after its share of the orders' discounts (O20), the discounts given (full price − revenue), its margin (revenue − buying cost, flagged when a cost is unknown) and its stock today; ranked by revenue, units, discounts or margin. Supplies are not listed | `GetProductsReportHandler` | `ProductReportsTest`, `product-reports.spec.js` |
| PR3 | **Product story**, month by month over the period: units sold, full-price sales and revenue; stock arrivals (every lot: purchases, supplier orders, inventory surpluses, returns) and departures (sales, inventory losses, use as supply); the stock at each month's end, worked back from today's stock | `GetProductReportHandler`, `ProductMovements` | `ProductReportsTest` |
| PR4 | **Discounts of a product**: every discount rule with a condition on the product or its type (any variant), cut to the period; « Remisé N jours sur M » counts the days covered by at least one of them, and each month shows the share of the full price given away | `DiscountRule::concerns()`, `ValidityPeriod::within()` | `ProductReportsTest` |
| PR5 | **Analysis** (« Décisions »): every article not archived (itself or its type) that has **arrived** by the period's end, arrival being the Europe/Paris day of its first stock lot (any lot), or of its first sale of the period if earlier. Supplies and products never stocked nor sold are left out | `Arrival` | `ArrivalTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR6 | **Exposure** = the period's unrefunded orders placed from its arrival to the period's end, or to its last sale when the period reaches today and nothing is left in stock (a product that sold out before the period has no exposure and is left out of its type's benchmark). **Pace** = units per 100 orders of exposure, to one decimal (none when the exposure is 0). **Too early to judge** under 150 orders since arrival, or under half of the period's orders | `Exposure`, `OrderCalendar` | `ExposureTest`, `OrderCalendarTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR7 | **ABC class** on the period's margin (revenue after its discount share − recorded cost), ranked by margin then name: A while the margin cumulated before the product is under 80 % of the total of the positive margins (losses do not shrink it), B under 95 %, else C; a margin of zero or less is C | `MarginClass` | `MarginClassTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR8 | **Quadrant** among the judged (not too early) products of its type: popular when its pace reaches 70 % of their average pace (rounded to one decimal); profitable when its margin per unit reaches 85 % of their Σ margin ÷ Σ units; star (both), workhorse (popular only), puzzle (profitable only), dog (neither). None when too early or unsold | `TypeBenchmark`, `Quadrant` | `TypeBenchmarkTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR9 | **Stock**, only when the period reaches today: on hand = stock left to sell (K18); cover = orders it lasts at its pace (on hand × exposure ÷ units, rounded down; none when unsold); out when nothing is left, low when the cover is under the orders of the period's busiest event, else ok | `StockLevel`, `Exposure::coverOrders()`, `StockPotential` | `StockLevelTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR10 | **Verdict**, only when the period reaches today, first match: too early → discontinue (nothing sold, or unpopular and class C) → restock ((popular or class A) and stock out or low) → reprice (workhorse) → watch (unpopular, not class A) → keep | `Verdict` | `VerdictTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR11 | **Per event**: units at each event with orders in the period, oldest first, and outside events; no figure for an event that ended before its arrival or started after its last sale once sold out. No trend is computed | `UnitsByEvent`, `SalesCalendar` | `UnitsByEventTest`, `SalesLedgerTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |

## API

| Use case | Endpoint |
|---|---|
| `GetProductsReport` | `GET /api/reports/products?period=12m\|YYYY\|all` → `{period, years, products, totals}` |
| `GetProductsAnalysis` | `GET /api/reports/products/analysis?period=…` → `{period: {choice, from, to, current}, orders, offEventOrders, events, busiestEvent, types, products, totals}`; `stock`, `coverOrders` and `verdict` are null when the period ends before today |
| `GetProductReport` | `GET /api/reports/products/{id}?period=…` → `{product, period, months, discounts, discountedDays}` |

## UI

- A one-sentence summary of the period (cashed, items sold, discounts given), then the **palmarès**: one bar per product — in « Encaissé », its length is the full-price sales, solid for what was cashed and hatched for what discounts gave away; the other rankings draw the chosen figure. Hover for the details.
- Clicking a product opens its story beside the list (above it on narrow screens): sales per month, stock movements (arrivals above the line, departures below, the month-end stock as a line), and the discount rules as spans over the period with each month's discounted share. Period and product live in the URL (`?period=`, `?product=`).
- A switcher under the summary picks the view, kept in `?view=`: `palmares` (default) or `decisions`; an unknown value shows the palmarès. The page loads the report and the analysis in the same round trip; on a period change the previous figures stay, dimmed, until the new ones arrive.
- **Décisions**: one tile per verdict (PR10) with its count, plus « Tous »; a tile filters the table (`?verdict=`, a verdict with no product is disabled) and the table lists every analysed product (PR5) with its verdict, ABC class, quadrant, units, pace per 100 orders, margin (flagged when a cost is unknown), unit margin, stock with its out / low badge, cover in orders and, on wide screens, a strip of units per event (« — » where it was not on sale, PR11). Search in `?q=`, sort in `?sort=` (`name`, `verdict`, `class`, `quadrant`, `units`, `pace`, `margin` by default, `unitMargin`, `onHand`, `cover`) and `?dir=` (`asc`, `desc`). When the period is over, a notice replaces the tiles and the verdict, stock and cover columns. Clicking a product opens its story beside the table on very wide screens (110rem and up), above it otherwise, so the table keeps its stock and cover columns in view. A `?sort=` the table does not know falls back to the margin.

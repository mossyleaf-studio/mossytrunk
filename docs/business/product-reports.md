# Product report (Bilan produits)

`/reports/products` (Bureau › « Bilan produits ») tells how the catalogue sold over a period, without touching the product pages.

| # | Rule | Where | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| PR1 | The period is « 12 derniers mois » (from the first day of the month eleven months ago to today, the default), a calendar year, or « Depuis le début » (from the first sale); days in Europe/Paris. Refunded orders are left out | `ReportPeriod` | `ProductReportsTest` |
| PR2 | **Palmarès**: every article sold in the period with its units, its sales at full price, its revenue after its share of the orders' discounts (O20), the discounts given (full price − revenue), its margin (revenue − buying cost, flagged when a cost is unknown) and its stock today; ranked by revenue, units, discounts or margin. Supplies are not listed | `GetProductsReportHandler` | `ProductReportsTest`, `product-reports.spec.js` |
| PR5 | **Analysis** (« Décisions »): every article not archived (itself or its type) that has **arrived** by the period's end, arrival being the Europe/Paris day of its first stock lot (any lot), or of its first sale of the period if earlier. Supplies and products never stocked nor sold are left out | `Arrival` | `ArrivalTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR6 | **Exposure** = the period's unrefunded orders placed from its arrival to the period's end, or to its last sale when the period reaches today and nothing is left in stock (a product that sold out before the period has no exposure and is left out of its type's benchmark). **Pace** = units per 100 orders of exposure, to one decimal (none when the exposure is 0). **Too early to judge** under 150 orders since arrival, or under half of the period's orders | `Exposure`, `OrderCalendar` | `ExposureTest`, `OrderCalendarTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR7 | **ABC class** on the period's margin (revenue after its discount share − recorded cost), ranked by margin then name: A while the margin cumulated before the product is under 80 % of the total of the positive margins (losses do not shrink it), B under 95 %, else C; a margin of zero or less is C | `MarginClass` | `MarginClassTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR8 | **Quadrant** among the judged (not too early) products of its type: popular when its pace reaches 70 % of their average pace, the average and then the limit each rounded to one decimal (an average of 9.1 gives 6.4); profitable when its margin per unit (margin ÷ units, to the cent) reaches 85 % of their average margin per unit (Σ margin ÷ Σ units), the average and then the limit each rounded to the cent, half up (2.24 € gives 1.90 €), so 70 % and 85 % of the averages shown give the limits shown; star (both), workhorse (popular only), puzzle (profitable only), dog (neither). None when too early or unsold | `TypeBenchmark`, `Quadrant` | `TypeBenchmarkTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR9 | **Stock**, only when the period reaches today: on hand = stock left to sell (K18); cover = orders it lasts at its pace (on hand × exposure ÷ units, rounded down; none when unsold); out when nothing is left, low when the cover is under the orders of the period's busiest event, else ok | `StockLevel`, `Exposure::coverOrders()`, `StockPotential` | `StockLevelTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR10 | **Verdict**, only when the period reaches today, first match: too early → discontinue (nothing sold, or unpopular and class C) → restock ((popular or class A) and stock out or low) → reprice (workhorse) → watch (unpopular, not class A) → keep; it follows from the reason (PR12) | `Verdict`, `Reason` | `ReasonTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR11 | **Per event**: units at each event with orders in the period, oldest first, and outside events; no figure for an event that ended before its arrival or started after its last sale once sold out. No trend is computed | `UnitsByEvent`, `SalesCalendar` | `UnitsByEventTest`, `SalesLedgerTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR12 | **Reason** of the verdict, only when the period reaches today, the first matching cause in the order of PR10: `too_few_orders` (under 150 orders since arrival) and `arrived_late` (then under half of the period's orders) → too early; `nothing_sold` (no unit sold) and `unpopular_tail` (unpopular and class C) → discontinue; `sold_out` (nothing left) and `low_cover` (cover under the busiest event's orders) → restock; `workhorse` → reprice; `unpopular` (not class A) → watch; `top_earner` (unpopular but class A) and `fine` → keep. A product with no quadrant counts as unpopular | `Reason` | `ReasonTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |
| PR13 | **Sold discounted or at full price**: a unit is sold discounted when its order line carries a share of a discount (O20), at full price otherwise; per product the full-price and discounted units (their sum is the units sold) and the full price of the discounted units, summed with the other figures in the database (no order read) | `ProductSales`, `ProductSalesLedger` | `SalesLedgerTest`, `ProductReportsTest`, `ProductsAnalysisTest` |

## API

| Use case | Endpoint |
|---|---|
| `GetProductsReport` | `GET /api/reports/products?period=12m\|YYYY\|all` → `{period, years, products, totals}`; a product gives `units`, `fullPriceUnits`, `discountedUnits`, `gross`, `discountedGross`, `discount`, `revenue`, `cost`, `margin` (cents), `unknownCost`, `onHand`; `totals` = `{units, discountedUnits, gross, revenue, discount, margin}` |
| `GetProductsAnalysis` | `GET /api/reports/products/analysis?period=…` → `{rules: {enoughOrders, popularPercent, profitablePercent, classAPercent, classBPercent}, period: {choice, from, to, current}, orders, offEventOrders, events, busiestEvent, types, products, totals}`; a type gives `judged`, `averagePace`, `popularFrom`, `averageUnitMargin`, `unitMarginFrom`; a product gives the sold figures of `GetProductsReport` plus `unitMargin`, `availableSince`, `exposure`, `ordersSinceArrival`, `per100Orders`, `class`, `quadrant`, `cumulativeMargin`, `onHand`, `coverOrders`, `stock`, `byEvent`, `offEvent`, `reason`, `verdict`; `totals` = `{units, discountedUnits, revenue, margin, onHand}`; `stock`, `coverOrders`, `reason` and `verdict` are null when the period ends before today |

## UI

- A one-sentence summary of the period (cashed, items sold, how many of them were discounted and what the discounts cost), then the **palmarès**: one bar per product — in « Encaissé », its length is the full-price sales, solid for what was cashed and hatched for what discounts gave away; in « Articles vendus », its length is the units, solid for those sold at full price and hatched for those sold discounted (PR13), each row saying « N vendus, dont M remisés »; the other rankings draw the chosen figure. Hover for the details.
- Clicking a product, in any view, opens its **decision sheet** beside the list (above it on narrow screens), built from the analysis already loaded (no request) and closed by its button. Period and product live in the URL (`?period=`, `?product=`). It gives the product's type, name and a link to its page; when the period is current, its verdict and one sentence for its reason (PR12) with its own numbers; then « Pourquoi ce verdict », one row per check (time on sale, pace, margin per unit, ABC class and rank, stock) with the product's figure, the limit, how the limit is obtained and the outcome in words; « D'où vient la marge », a receipt opening with « N vendus : X au plein tarif, Y remisés » and a two-part bar, then full price − discounts (their amount, average per discounted unit and share of their full price) = cashed − buying cost = margin, ÷ units sold = margin per unit, with a warning when a cost is unknown; and « Où il s'est vendu », one bar per event of the period and outside events, « — » where it was not on sale. When the period is over there is no verdict and no stock check, and a notice says so. A product the analysis does not list (archived) gets the receipt from the palmarès row and a line saying archived products are not analysed.
- A switcher under the summary picks the view, kept in `?view=`: `palmares` (default), `decisions`, `concentration`, `matrix`, `stock` or `events`; an unknown value shows the palmarès. The page loads the report and the analysis in the same round trip; on a period change the previous figures stay, dimmed, until the new ones arrive. Every view has a « Comment c'est calculé » block, closed by default, which explains its figures with the real numbers of the page (the rules, the types' averages and limits, the busiest event).
- **Décisions**: one tile per verdict (PR10) with its count, plus « Tous »; a tile filters the table (`?verdict=`, a verdict with no product is disabled) and the table lists every analysed product (PR5) with its verdict, ABC class, quadrant, units, pace per 100 orders, margin (flagged when a cost is unknown), unit margin, stock with its out / low badge, cover in orders and, on wide screens, a strip of units per event (« — » where it was not on sale, PR11). Search in `?q=`, sort in `?sort=` (`name`, `verdict`, `class`, `quadrant`, `units`, `pace`, `margin` by default, `unitMargin`, `onHand`, `cover`) and `?dir=` (`asc`, `desc`). The column headers say what they hold (« Rythme pour 100 commandes », « Marge par unité », « Tient commandes ») and a verdict badge's tooltip gives the product's own reason sentence. The block says what pace, class, quadrant, « tient » and each verdict mean and which limits they use. When the period is over, a notice replaces the tiles and the verdict, stock and cover columns. The sheet opens beside the table on very wide screens (110rem and up), above it otherwise, so the table keeps its stock and cover columns in view. A `?sort=` the table does not know falls back to the margin.
- **Concentration**: a sentence giving the class A and class C products and their real share of the margin (« N produits sur M apportent 89 % de la marge ; K n'en apportent que 4 % »), the cumulative margin curve in rank order with the 80 % and 95 % lines, a legend per class (count, margin, share) and two segmented bars (margin, products). The class comes from the API (PR7); hover or focus a product on the curve for its rank, margin, cumulated share and class. On a touch screen each product keeps a 1.5rem slot, so a long list scrolls sideways. Its block says what the margin is and how the classes are cut, with the marks read from the rules.
- **Matrice**: one panel per product type with judged products: pace per 100 orders across, unit margin up, the two thresholds drawn as lines with their values and the four quadrant names in the corners; a dot is a product (click to open it). Products without a quadrant (too early, unsold or in a type too small) are listed apart under « Hors matrice » with their verdict; when the period is over, a notice says verdicts only read up to today. Each panel names its two lines and how they come from the type's averages (« 70 % du rythme moyen des Keychain, 2,0 », « 85 % de leur marge moyenne par unité, 8,55 € »); the block says what the margin per unit is and what the four names mean.
- **Stock**, only when the period reaches today (a notice otherwise): the units in stock, how many products are out or low, the busiest event's orders, then one row per product with sold and in-stock bars, its state badge and the orders its stock lasts. Sort in `?stockSort=` (`shortest` by default, `longest`, `onHand`); the first 15 rows show, the rest on demand. Its block says how « tient N commandes » is obtained and what « stock court » is compared with (the busiest event, by name).
- **Événements**: a heat table, one row per product (most sold first, 15 shown, the rest on demand), one column per event of the period linked to the event, « Hors événement » when some orders were sold outside events, then the total. Cells give the units, tinted in three levels by units per 100 orders against the table's highest rate; « — » where the product was not on sale. Its block says what the tint measures (units per 100 orders of the event) and what « — » and « 0 » mean.

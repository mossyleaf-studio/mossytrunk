# Website import

The studio sells on **its own website** (mossyleaf.studio, project `~/Sites/mossyleaf-studio/website`), paid with SumUp's hosted checkout. MossyTrunk reads the **paid orders** of that shop and runs them through the generic [import](imports.md), like Etsy's receipts, and **publishes its catalogue with the stock** to the shop: MossyTrunk is the source of truth of what the shop sells (reference, names, type, variants, price, stock); the website adds photos, texts and visibility. This page lists what is specific to the website. MossyTrunk is always the client: the website never calls it, and no customer data leaves the website.

Configuration: « Paramètres › Services connectés › Ajouter un service › Site web »: the **address** of the site (`https://mossyleaf.studio`) and the **token** the site expects (its `MOSSYTRUNK_TOKEN`, stored encrypted here). There is no consent page: the service is « Prêt » as soon as both are typed.
Defaults: sales **online** (no event), unknown items **ask me**, line prices **listed**.

Code: `src/Infrastructure/Connector/Website/` — `WebsiteConnector` (`CataloguePublishing`, so a `SalesConnector` too), `WebsiteGateway`, `WebsiteApiGateway`, `WebsitePayloadMapper`, `WebsiteAccess` (address, token and the label to name the service in errors), `FakeWebsiteGateway` (test env: fixture `tests/Fixtures/website/orders.json`, one paid order and one cancelled, whose line names are those of no product so that only the SKU can match them; it remembers the last catalogue published and answers what a site holding it would: created, updated, retired; a service whose address is `https://down.example` cannot read the orders and one at `https://refusing.example` has its catalogue refused, the two failures of W16). Publishing: `src/Application/Integration/` — port `CataloguePublishing`, `StockedProduct` / `StockedItem`, `CatalogueChanges`, `PublishCatalogue/PublishCatalogueHandler`, and `Presentation/Api/Integration/PublishCatalogueController`.

## What is read from the website

- `GET {address}/integrations/orders` with `Authorization: Bearer {token}` → `{"orders": [{"id", "reference", "placedAt", "lines": [{"sku", "name", "variant", "unitPrice", "quantity"}], "goods", "shipping", "cancelledAt"}]}`: every paid order ever, oldest first, amounts in integer cents. The website answers 401 to a wrong token and 404 while its integration is switched off.
- Mapping: `id` = external id, `reference` as given (the id when it is blank); line = `sku` (external reference and SKU), `name`, `variant`, `quantity`, `unitPrice`; charged = `goods`; shipping = `shipping`; payment « Carte »; fee 0; cancelled on `cancelledAt`.

## What is published to the website

- `PUT {address}/integrations/catalogue` with `Authorization: Bearer {token}` and `{"products": [{"id", "reference", "name", "category", "displayName", "price", "variants": [{"name", "sku", "stock"}]}]}` → `{"created", "updated", "retired"}`. `id` is the product's ULID in MossyTrunk (the key on the website: a reference may change), `category` its type, `displayName` « {type} {name} », `price` integer cents, `stock` 0 or more.
- One `variants` entry per sellable item, with the SKU of I5: `{"name": "A4", "sku": "<reference>-A4"}` for each active variant, or the single `{"name": null, "sku": "<reference>"}` for a product without variants. The orders read back carry these SKUs (W5).
- The website answers 422 with a `detail` when it refuses the catalogue (empty list, an id, reference or SKU sent twice, more than 2000 products or 50 variants for one product, a field too long), 401 and 404 as for the orders.

## Development

`when@dev` (`config/services.yaml`) loosens the two container parameters so that a development machine reaches the website's dev stack at `http://host.docker.internal:8094`: `app.website.address_pattern` also takes `http` and a port, and `app.website.allowed_private_subnets` lets the private IPv4 ranges through. Test, e2e and production keep W1 and W2 as written; test and e2e use `FakeWebsiteGateway`.

## UI

The service shows in the picker and the generic form of « Paramètres › Services connectés » (the address carries a hint and the message of W1), is imported by « Importer les ventes » and by « Synchroniser » on Commandes, and its orders are grouped under « Boutique Site web ».

Its row carries « **Publier le catalogue** » once the service is « Prêt ». The confirmation says that the website's catalogue is replaced by MossyTrunk's (products no longer in MossyTrunk are withdrawn from the shop, new ones arrive hidden) and that the site's sales are imported just before. While the publication runs, the button shows it and « Importer les ventes » of that service is disabled (and « Publier le catalogue » while its sales are imported), so that the same sales are not imported twice at once. One message then gives the new, updated and withdrawn products, the orders imported before sending, the products the import created (W16), and the orders that could not be imported. « Synchroniser » does not publish.
